Before bed
  1. Wet the H10 electrodes and put the strap on.
  2. iPad: Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never. Plug it in.
  3. Open this page in Bluefy, tap Connect & start, pick your H10.
  4. Tap Dim screen. Don't switch apps until morning.
The page saves a backup every minute, so if Bluefy closes you can still export what was recorded. Close the Polar app first so it doesn't hold the H10 connection.
